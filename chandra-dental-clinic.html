Your dentist

Dr. Umesh Chandra

PRINCIPAL DENTIST · CHANDRA DENTAL CLINIC, BASTI

Dr. Umesh Chandra treats the full range of dental needs at the clinic, from a child's first check-up to root canal therapy performed with an endomotor for greater precision and comfort. Every visit starts the same way: a clear look at what's going on, and a straightforward plan for fixing it.

Appointments are recommended so your visit is ready to go the moment you arrive — call ahead and the team will fit you in as soon as possible, sooner still if it's an emergency.
